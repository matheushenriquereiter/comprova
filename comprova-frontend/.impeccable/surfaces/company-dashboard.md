## Direction contract

**STORY:** The HR recruiter needs a high-density, highly legible view of their active job pipeline to quickly assess candidate volume before creating new technical assessments.
**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A top navigation bar (logo left, profile right). Below it, a primary action bar with a "New Posting" button. The main content is a full-width, clean data table listing Job Postings (Title, Status, Expires At, Candidate Count, Skills).
**SIGNATURE INTERACTION:** Clicking "New Posting" opens a 3-section Popup Modal. The sections cover: 1) Basic Job Details, 2) Requirements, and 3) An AI-Generated Technical Assessment Questions preview.
**FORM:** Top Nav + Full-width Data Table (User-pinned decision).
**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
