## Direction contract

**STORY:** The HR recruiter clicks into a job posting to review applied candidates. They use a clean data table to scan applicants, but rely on an always-available floating AI chat assistant to intelligently filter, rank, and query the talent pool.
**FIRST VIEWPORT:** The page sits within the established Dashboard Layout. It displays the Job Title and summary metrics at the top, followed by a full-width data table of candidates (Name, Match Score, Application Date, Status). A floating chat widget sits in the bottom right corner.
**SIGNATURE INTERACTION:** Opening the floating AI chat allows the recruiter to type natural language prompts (e.g., "Filter to candidates strong in React"). The chat responds and dynamically updates/filters the data table behind it.
**FORM:** Data table on top, with a floating/collapsible AI chat assistant (User-pinned decision).
**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
