# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary User: HR Recruiter / Hiring Manager**
- **Situation:** They have an open vacancy and need a reliable way to verify if applicants actually possess the technical skills listed on their resumes. They lack the time to manually create, administer, and grade custom technical assessments for a large volume of applicants.
- **Job They Are Doing:** Automating the technical triage process. They input the role's required competencies, minimum acceptable levels, and weightings (e.g., Java 30%, SQL 20%). They then use the system to automatically generate, administer, and grade technical tests via AI, ultimately reviewing a filtered shortlist of pre-validated candidates (Score de Aderência) to make a final hiring decision.

**Secondary User: The Candidate (Candidato)**
- **Situation:** They are applying for a specific vacancy and must transition their skills from "self-declared" to "validated" to pass the initial automated HR filter.
- **Job They Are Doing:** Executing the AI-generated technical assessment (multiple-choice, open questions, and practical coding/scripting challenges) directly in the platform to prove their proficiency, generate an auditable score, and reach the final review stage with the hiring manager.

## Product Purpose

ComProva automates the technical triage process for recruitment. It provides a reliable way for non-technical HR teams to verify applicants' technical skills through AI-generated and AI-graded assessments, outputting a validated score ("Score de Aderência") for final candidate review.

## Positioning

ComProva enables zero-bottleneck technical recruitment by allowing non-technical HR teams to accurately and automatically validate a candidate's actual engineering skills before a human interview takes place.

## Operating Context

The system sits between the initial application phase and the human interview phase. HR inputs competency requirements to generate tests; candidates take these tests on the platform; HR reviews the AI-graded results to shortlist candidates.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Generates tests based on competencies, levels, and weightings (e.g., Java 30%, SQL 20%).
- Administers multiple-choice, open questions, and practical coding/scripting challenges.
- Auto-grades candidates via AI and provides a "Score de Aderência".
- Currently a pure greenfield project with no strict constraints yet.
